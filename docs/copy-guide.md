# Copy guide

Zend users shouldn't need to know how money moves. Anything they read, in your app, your labels,
and your cards, should sound like messaging, not banking or crypto.

## Avoid

Sui, blockchain, chain, on-chain, crypto, token, USDC, stablecoin, wallet, wallet address,
gas, network fee, transaction, signature, key, seed, escrow, contract, zkLogin.

Zend rejects the main ones in manifest labels and card text.

## Say instead

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| wallet / balance | your balance, your Zend |
| transfer | send, pay, Zend |
| claim | collect |
| escrow / contract-held | held safely until the rules say who gets it |
| transaction failed | That didn't go through. Try again. |
| sign the transaction | confirm it's you |

Show amounts in Naira where you can. Keep labels short (chat actions ≤ 24 chars, buttons ≤ 20).
